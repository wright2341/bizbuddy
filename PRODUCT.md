# BizBuddy

BizBuddy helps small-business owners automate routine customer replies and
back-office follow-ups across connected channels. Owners need a fast path from
signup to a correct, business-specific reply: choose AI, add confirmed knowledge,
test an answer, activate chat. They should see model access, usage and costs.

Core workflows: unified inbox, customer records, business knowledge, safe AI
replies, order capture and owner confirmation, approved invoice reminders, daily
summaries and stock visibility. Unknown answers and payment decisions hand off
to the owner. Product facts come from saved data, never invented claims.

Users are merchants, often operating on phones and with little API experience.
Use the incumbent violet/lime/lavender identity, menu and landing artwork. Avoid
blocking ordinary workspace access; unfinished setup is resumable.

This installation is a prototype with owner-created test accounts. Managed AI
is available by default to every current and new account on the Free plan, capped
at 100 requests per account per UTC calendar month. Merchant-owned encrypted
OpenAI, Groq or Gemini connections remain optional. Platform pricing is free early
access unless the owner approves paid prices. Provider charges remain separate;
no subscription checkout is enabled without a payment provider.
